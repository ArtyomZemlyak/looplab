## What is genuinely good (and must survive the fixing)

The branch's dominant pattern is the right one: money/policy rules hoisted into named, testable
objects (`_interrupted_stream_is_salvageable`, `salvaged_lengths`, `reasoning_depth_keys`,
`is_guarded_abort`, `stop_account.py` as a read-only leaf); position-keyed fences replaced by
receipt-keyed ones (`_proposal_receipt_fence`); `defer_inband_error` changes one behavior by
REORDERING instead of forking five; the meter tests drive real subprocesses, raw sockets and
SO_LINGER resets rather than pinning source; and the marker discipline itself worked — the
2026-08-25 review's 26 markers were closed by deletion with evidence, and five standing markers
honestly describe defects this review re-confirmed. The failures found here are concentrated in
the two places the process predicts: hand-synchronized copies of one rule (the regime key, the
guarded-abort literal, the slot set), and merge conflicts resolved by keeping both sides (the
duplicated method, the duplicated report tail, the corrupted anchors, the red suite).
